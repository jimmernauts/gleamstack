## Summary

Checked first-party documentation/repositories as of **2026-08-22**. The strongest local-first shortlist candidates are **Evolu** and **Fireproof**; **TinyBase** is a good low-level prototype if Mealstack is willing to supply its own sync backend. **Replicache is maintained only in maintenance mode and should be treated as legacy**, while **Zero is GA but explicitly not local-first and does not support offline writes**. Automerge Repo, Jazz/cojson, LiveStore, and vlcn/cr-sqlite remain viable prototypes with materially different maturity/plumbing risks.

No candidate other than Evolu and Fireproof has a first-party, affirmative E2EE story in the reviewed material. “Full local” means the complete app-owned dataset can reside locally; query-driven products may intentionally keep only a subset. Stability labels below use explicit first-party labels where available; otherwise “not stated” is unresolved, not a production-readiness claim.

### Unresolved facts to close before a production choice

- Exact hosted pricing/support SLAs for Zero, Jazz, Evolu cloud relay, and Fireproof Cloud.
- Formal stability/compatibility guarantees for Evolu, Jazz/cojson, and Fireproof.
- Production-tested E2EE and key-recovery details for Jazz, plus exact Fireproof gateway encryption guarantees.
- Portable restore/import semantics: especially Evolu history, Automerge Repo storage, Jazz data, and Fireproof CAR/S3 recovery.
- Whether Mealstack needs hard deletes; Evolu’s normal row deletion is intentionally soft.
